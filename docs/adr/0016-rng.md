# ADR 0016: Random numbers for the statistics contract

Date: 2026-10-26. Status: accepted.

## Context

The admission gate (SPEC "Gates") rests on a paired bootstrap whose interval must be reproducible
from a recorded seed by anyone, years later, on any platform: a gate decision is an auditable
record, and two people checking it must draw the same resamples. Rust's `rand` crate changes its
range-sampling algorithm across minor versions, and `StdRng` is explicitly not stable across
versions.

## Decision

- The generator is ChaCha20 (`rand_chacha::ChaCha20Rng`), a specified stream cipher, seeded with
  `SeedableRng::seed_from_u64` from the matrix's `bootstrap_seed`, a 64-bit unsigned decimal.
- `rand` and `rand_chacha` are pinned exactly in the workspace (`=0.9.5`, `=0.9.0`). Bumping them
  is a contract change: the stored fixture under `fixtures/stats/` must be regenerated and the
  change recorded here.
- Draws are integers over the family index through `rand::Rng::random_range(0..families)`, one per
  selected family, in order, `families` draws per resample, resamples in order. Nothing else is
  drawn from the stream.
- Canonical ordering before any draw: families sorted by id, items sorted by id within a family,
  and only families holding at least one expected-fail item.
- Percentiles use the Hyndman and Fan type 7 quantile on the sorted resample estimands;
  Bonferroni-adjusted endpoints `0.025 / m` and `1 − 0.025 / m` for `m` hypotheses; an expected
  tail of fewer than 20 resamples below the lower percentile is `inconclusive`.
- Binomial proportions carry the Wilson score interval at `z = 1.959963984540054`.

## Alternatives

- `StdRng` or the thread RNG: not reproducible across `rand` versions, unseeded in the latter.
- A hand-rolled generator: nothing to gain over a specified cipher, and one more thing to get
  wrong.
- Floating-point draws scaled to indices: platform-dependent rounding and modulo bias.

## Consequences

`fixtures/stats/case-1.toml` stores the first resamples' indices and the interval for its seed;
`cargo test -p dejaview-eval -- stats` reproduces them bit for bit. Every gate result records its
seed, resample count and hypothesis count. `rand` upgrades are deliberate.
