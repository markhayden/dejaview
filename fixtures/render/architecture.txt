Rules:
1. SF1 (must hold; judges candidate; consults source) Permanent structure (walls, floors, ceilings, columns, pools, roads, built landmarks) keeps its shape, position and extent relative to the source. If this is hidden behind something else, say `hidden`; that is recorded, not unsure.

3. SF3 (allowed; on candidate) Furniture, people, vehicles, decoration, textiles, lighting and vegetation may change, appear or disappear. This states what may change; it waives no obligation above.
4. SF4 (observe; on candidate) Any other change is noted with its location. Report such things as `observed` findings; they never change the verdict.
