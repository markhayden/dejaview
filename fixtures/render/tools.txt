
Tools. Use them to look closer before deciding; every call is logged. Refer to images and frames by their ids.
- fake_regions (Cheap, about 0s): Test double: returns scripted change regions for an image.
  When to use: Only in tests.
  When not to use: Anywhere real.
  Output: Each region is a pointer, not a verdict.
- regions (Expensive, about 25s): Finds regions that changed between an aligned pair, with high recall.
  When to use: After align succeeds on a source and candidate, before judging structure.
  When not to use: Without a source image, or on a pair align could not register.
  Output: Each region is a pointer, not a verdict. Inspect regions with view before deciding.
  Prerequisites: `against` must be an aligned frame of the source relative to `image`.
  Requires: align
  Pairs with: view, classes
